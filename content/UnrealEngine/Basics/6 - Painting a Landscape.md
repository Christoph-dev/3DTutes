---
title: 6 - Painting a Landscape
---


1. Setting up the Material
a. Open the **Content Drawer**

b. Go to your **Art Folder**, right click and create a new material called **M_MyLandscape**  
c. Open your new material. Right click and search for **LandscapeLayerBlend**. Click to add.  
d. Connect it to the **Base Colour**.  
e. For a stylised look, it can help to reduce **Specula**r to <0.2 and increase **Roughness** to 1.0  

![[LandScapeMaterial1.png]]

2. Adding Layers in the Material

We want 3 layers. (e.g. Grass, Dirt, Rock)  
a. Click on the LandScapeLayerBlend node and in the details panel, click this plus three times.  

![[LandScapeMaterial2.png]]

b. You don't have to choose Grass, Dirt, Rock, but make sure you name each.  
![[LandScapeMaterial3.png]]

c. Now hold down 3, and click into the graph editor to create three colours.  
d. Double click the colour node and choose applicable colours for your layers, e.g. Grass = Green.  
![[LandScapeMaterial4.png]]

e. Save and Close your material.  

3. Adding Material to the Landscape.  
a. In your level, click your Landscape.  
b. Scroll down the details until you find Landscape Material  
c. Set your new Landscape Material  
![[LandScapeMaterial5.png]]

4. Initialising the Landscape Material  
a. In the Landscape Mode Menu, Go to the Paint tab.  
b. Click this button in the Target Layers area to create your layers.  
![[LandScapeMaterial6.png]]

c. Click the Plus on each layer, to save the brush information we add on the next step.  
![[LandScapeMaterial7.png]]

Saving it in the Maps Folder is fine. Hit Save for each.  
![[LandScapeMaterial8.png]]

5. Painting the Landscape  
Highlight a layer and start painting!  
![[LandScapeMaterial9.png]]
