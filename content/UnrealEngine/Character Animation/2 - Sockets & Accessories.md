title: 2 - Sockets & Accessories
---
To add our face to our dino or for equipping him with any other accessories, we need sockets.

1. Open up SK_Dino.
2. Find JNT_Head in the Skeleton Tree.
3. Right click JNT_Head and click 'Add Socket'.  
![[Anim6.png]]

4. Now go back to the BP_Dino that we set up in the first lesson.
5. In the components list, click Add and then Static Mesh  
![[Anim7.png]]

6. The static mesh should be a child of the CharacterMesh.
7. In the details panel of the static mesh, change the model to Dino_Face.
8. In the same details panel, find the 'Parent Socket' section and choose our socket.
![[Anim8.png]]
9. Rotate the face by **\[0, 0, 90]**
